# Exploration-of-AI-Use-Case-Case-study-on-self-driving-cars

## AIM

To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.
## THEORY      

A **self-driving car** is an autonomous vehicle that uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) to perceive its surroundings, understand road conditions, make driving decisions, and control the vehicle with minimal or no human intervention.

Self-driving cars use technologies such as **Computer Vision, Machine Learning (ML), Deep Learning (DL), LiDAR, Radar, GPS, Sensor Fusion, and Path Planning** to detect objects, recognize traffic signs and lanes, predict the movement of other road users, and determine a safe path.

AI enables the vehicle to continuously analyze sensor data and make decisions such as **steering, accelerating, braking, changing lanes, and avoiding obstacles**. The main goal of autonomous driving is to improve road safety, driving efficiency, and transportation convenience.

## WORKING PRINCIPLE

The basic working process of an AI-based self-driving car is:

**Road Environment → Sensors → Data Processing → Object Detection → Sensor Fusion → Decision Making → Path Planning → Vehicle Control → Vehicle Movement**

1. The vehicle uses **cameras, LiDAR, radar, GPS, and other sensors** to collect information about its surroundings.
2. **Computer Vision** processes camera images to identify lanes, traffic signs, vehicles, pedestrians, and other objects.
3. **Sensor Fusion** combines data from multiple sensors to obtain a more accurate understanding of the environment.
4. **AI/ML models** detect objects and predict the possible movement of vehicles and pedestrians.
5. The system determines the vehicle's **position, direction, and surrounding road conditions**.
6. **Path Planning** selects a safe and suitable route based on the detected environment.
7. The **Decision-Making system** determines actions such as steering, accelerating, braking, or changing lanes.
8. **Vehicle Control** sends commands to the steering, accelerator, and braking systems.
9. The vehicle performs the required action and continuously repeats the process using newly collected sensor data.

## TECHNOLOGIES USED IN SELF-DRIVING CARS

1. **Computer Vision:** Uses camera images to identify lanes, vehicles, pedestrians, traffic signs, and road conditions.
2. **Machine Learning (ML):** Learns patterns from driving data to improve decision-making and object recognition.
3. **Deep Learning (DL):** Used for complex tasks such as object detection, image classification, and prediction.
4. **LiDAR:** Uses laser pulses to create a 3D representation of the surrounding environment.
5. **Radar:** Detects the distance, speed, and position of surrounding objects, especially in poor visibility conditions.
6. **GPS and GNSS:** Helps determine the vehicle's location and support navigation.
7. **Sensor Fusion:** Combines data from cameras, LiDAR, radar, GPS, and other sensors to improve environmental perception.
8. **Object Detection and Tracking:** Identifies and continuously tracks vehicles, pedestrians, cyclists, and other objects.
9. **Path Planning:** Determines a safe and efficient path for the vehicle to follow.
10. **Decision Making:** Determines actions such as braking, acceleration, steering, and lane changing.
11. **Vehicle Control Systems:** Converts AI decisions into physical actions through steering, braking, and acceleration systems.
12. **High-Definition Maps:** Provides detailed information about roads, lanes, intersections, and other road features.

## CHARACTERISTICS OF AI-BASED SELF-DRIVING CARS

1. **Autonomous Navigation:** Navigates roads and reaches destinations with minimal human intervention.
2. **Environment Perception:** Detects and understands vehicles, pedestrians, lanes, traffic signs, and obstacles.
3. **Real-Time Decision Making:** Makes driving decisions based on continuously changing road conditions.
4. **Obstacle Detection:** Identifies obstacles and takes appropriate actions to avoid collisions.
5. **Lane Detection:** Detects road lanes and maintains the vehicle within the appropriate lane.
6. **Traffic Sign Recognition:** Identifies and interprets traffic signs and signals.
7. **Path Planning:** Calculates a safe and efficient path based on the surrounding environment.
8. **Adaptive Control:** Adjusts steering, speed, acceleration, and braking according to road conditions.
9. **Sensor Fusion:** Combines information from multiple sensors for improved environmental awareness.
10. **Continuous Perception:** Continuously monitors the surroundings and updates driving decisions in real time.

## RESULT

The application of **Artificial Intelligence in self-driving cars** was studied. The role of **Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Sensor Fusion, LiDAR, Radar, and Path Planning** in perceiving the environment, making driving decisions, and controlling the vehicle was explored.
